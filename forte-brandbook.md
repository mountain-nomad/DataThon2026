## 1. Brand Identity

The logo's contemporary and dynamic design expresses a positive and future-oriented vision. **The icon's rising shape and colour shading, reminiscent of a sunrise, suggest a new beginning, a period of growth and progress.**

## 2. Logotype

### 2.1 Logotype colour

The master logotype is **Forte Magenta**. It reads clearly on light and dark surfaces. Keep clear space around the mark and never recolour it outside the approved set.

### 4.2 Gradients

- **Primary gradient:** Dark Magenta → Coral: `linear-gradient(90deg, #4A0221 0%, #FC9385 100%)` *(page data)*
- **Secondary gradient:** Magenta → Pink: `linear-gradient(90deg, #A31551 0%, #F03785 100%)`, so **Pink = `#F03785`**. The page gives no RGB or CMYK for Pink *(page data)*.
- **Forte Presentation key visual:** `linear-gradient(115deg, #4A0221 0%, #B8335F 55%, #FC9385 100%)`. The logo icon's own gradient uses the same mid tone: `#F99184 → #B8335F → #A51652` *(page data)*.
- **Business Presentation key visual:** `linear-gradient(160deg, #1464A5 0%, #072146 100%)` *(page data)*.

## 5. Typography

**Typeface:** Open Sans

Open Sans is the brand typeface across every product and surface: a humanist sans that stays legible from caption to display.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Family | Open Sans |
| Weights | 300 (Light) · 400 (Regular) · 600 (Semibold) · 700 (Bold) · 800 (Extrabold) |
| Usage | UI · display · body · captions |

Download: https://forte.kz/docs/open-sans.zip
